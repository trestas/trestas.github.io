Privacy Policy

Nonet: Block Puzzle (Android app, package com.solocodelab.blockpuzzle) · Effective 15 October 2026

Nonet: Block Puzzle is a block puzzle game. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, location, contacts or photos, and you do not need an account. This policy explains the little data the app does use, why, and how you can control it.

Who is responsible

SoloCodeLab, the developer of the app, is responsible for its data. Contact: solocodelab@gmail.com.

Data stored only on your device

Your games in progress, best scores, level stars, daily streak and settings are saved on your device. They are not sent to us and are not included in Android backups.

Legal bases (EEA and UK)

  • Personalised ads: your consent.
  • Non-personalised ads and invite abuse prevention: our legitimate interest in offering a free, fair and working game, balanced by the choices above.
  • Invite rewards and purchases: performance of the contract with you.

How long data is kept

Data on your device stays until you clear the app's data or uninstall it. Invite data is kept while you use the app and deleted within 12 months after your last activity, or sooner if you ask. Google keeps ad data under its own policies.

Children

The game is meant for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. Ads are limited to content rated for general audiences (G and PG). If you believe a child under 13 has sent us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete your data, object to its processing or withdraw consent at any time. For invite data, send us your invite code. You may also complain to your data protection authority; in Lithuania this is the State Data Protection Inspectorate (vdai.lrv.lt).

Security

Data sent by the app travels encrypted (HTTPS). Invite data can only be changed by our server rules, not directly by other players.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
