Privacy Policy

JazzIn: Block Puzzle (Android app, package com.solocodelab.blockpuzzle) · Effective 21 November 2026

JazzIn: Block Puzzle is a block puzzle game. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, location, contacts or photos, and you do not need an account. This policy explains the little data the app does use, why, and how you can control it.

Who is responsible

SoloCodeLab, the developer of the app, is responsible for its data. Contact: solocodelab@gmail.com.

Data stored only on your device

Your games in progress, best scores, level stars, daily streak and settings are saved on your device. They are not sent to us and are not included in Android backups.

Our own ads for other apps and update checks

When the ad network has no ad to show, JazzIn: Block Puzzle may show a banner on the home screen or a short full-screen playable ad for one of our own apps (JazzIn Sudoku, JazzIn TrackOut or JazzIn Boulder) that is not yet installed on your device. To do this the app downloads its ad settings from trestas.github.io/ads/, a public page we host on GitHub Pages (like any website, it can see your IP address when you download). The app checks which of our apps are installed only on your device and sends this list nowhere. Impressions and taps are recorded in Firebase Analytics as events with the target app, format and style; no personal data is added. A tap opens the app's Google Play page with a source tag; if you install it, the other app learns once, through the Google Play Install Referrer, that the install came from this promotion (source, campaign and format names only, no personal data). Likewise, if you installed this game from an ad in one of our other apps, the game records that once, using the same tags. Players who bought “Remove ads” or have Premium do not see these ads. Separately, when the app starts it asks Google Play whether a newer version is available and, if so, offers to install it; this exchange is only between your device and Google Play. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in promoting our own apps and keeping the app up to date.

Legal bases (EEA and UK)

  • Personalised ads: your consent.
  • Non-personalised ads, analytics, crash reports, our own ads for other apps, update checks and invite abuse prevention: our legitimate interest in offering a free, fair, working and improving game, balanced by the choices above.
  • Invite rewards and purchases: performance of the contract with you.

How long data is kept

Data on your device stays until you clear the app's data or uninstall it. Invite data is kept while you use the app and deleted within 12 months after your last activity, or sooner if you ask. Analytics data is kept for a limited period set in Firebase (no longer than 14 months). Google keeps ad data under its own policies.

Children

The game is meant for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. Ads are limited to content rated for general audiences (G and PG). If you believe a child under 13 has sent us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete your data, object to its processing or withdraw consent at any time. For invite data, send us your invite code. You may also complain to your data protection authority; in Lithuania this is the State Data Protection Inspectorate (vdai.lrv.lt).

Security

Data sent by the app travels encrypted (HTTPS). Invite data can only be changed by our server rules, not directly by other players.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
