Privacy Policy

JazzIn: TrackOut (Android app, package com.solocodelab.trackout) · Effective 10 November 2026

JazzIn: TrackOut is a logic puzzle game. The game does not ask for your name, email, phone number, location, contacts or photos, and you do not need an account. This policy explains the little data the app does use, why, and how you can control it.

Who is responsible

SoloCodeLab, the developer of the app, is responsible for its data. Contact: solocodelab@gmail.com.

Data stored only on your device

Your progress, stars, statistics, daily streak, hints and settings are saved on your device. They are not sent to us. If Android backup is switched on for your device, Android may copy your game progress to your own Google account so it can be restored on a new device. Advertising, consent and Firebase files are not part of that backup. The daily reminder is a notification scheduled on your device; the app asks for notification permission only if you switch the reminder on, and the reminder sends nothing to us.

Sharing, writing to us and reporting names

When you share a result or an invitation, the text is sent by the app you choose (for example a messenger); we do not see it. The support and rating windows can open your own email app with a prepared message to solocodelab@gmail.com, or the TrackOut page on Google Play. The game itself sends nothing in either case. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and use it only to answer you and improve the game. When you report a ranking name, the prepared email contains that name, the board and the points; you can edit it before sending. We keep such messages for as long as needed to reply and delete them on request.

Legal bases (EEA and UK)

  • Personalised ads: your consent.
  • Non-personalised ads, analytics, Remote Config and rankings: our legitimate interest in offering a free, working and improving game, balanced by the choices above.
  • Purchases and support payments: performance of the contract with you.
  • Invitations: our legitimate interest in running the invitation feature and preventing abuse; performance of the contract for the credits you earn.
  • Emails you send us: our legitimate interest in answering you.

How long data is kept

Data on your device stays until you clear the app's data or uninstall it. Analytics data is kept for a limited period set in Firebase (no longer than 14 months). Ranking entries stay while the ranking is shown, or until you ask us to delete them. Invitation records are kept while credits can still be used, or until you ask us to delete them. Google keeps ad data under its own policies.

Children

JazzIn: TrackOut is meant for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. Ads are limited to content rated for general audiences. If you believe a child under 13 has sent us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete your data, object to its processing or withdraw consent at any time. For ranking entries, send us your name and the approximate date. You may also complain to your data protection authority; in Lithuania this is the State Data Protection Inspectorate (vdai.lrv.lt).

Security

Data sent by the app travels encrypted (HTTPS). Ranking rules only let each anonymous player change their own entry, and only to a better result.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
