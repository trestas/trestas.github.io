Every exit changes the board. Draw a route to each matching exit, then send the shape on its way. Ordinary tiles disappear after a shape leaves, solid platforms stay, and a path that works for one shape may leave another stranded. Who should leave first?

How to play

The basics

Touch a shape and drag to draw a path to its exit (or tap the next tile to continue the path), then press “Exit”. Plain tiles on the path disappear, solid pads stay, so other shapes can cross them. Get every shape out. Undo is unlimited.

New tiles in later chapters

Double tiles last two journeys, and arrows can be left only in the direction they point.

Premium and support

The game is free with ads. One purchase removes ads and adds daily hints, the daily board archive and extra themes. Voluntary support does not unlock anything.
