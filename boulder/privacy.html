Privacy Policy

JazzIn Boulder: Gem Puzzle (Android app, package com.solocodelab.boulder) · Effective 19 November 2026

JazzIn Boulder: Gem Puzzle is a puzzle game. The game does not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts or photos, and you do not need an account. This policy explains the little data the app does use, why, and how you can control it.

Who is responsible

SoloCodeLab, the developer of the JazzIn games, is responsible for the app's data. Contact: solocodelab@gmail.com.

Data stored only on your device

Your progress (unlocked levels, best scores, the saved game) and settings are saved on your device. They are not sent to us. If Android backup is switched on for your device, Android may copy the app's data to your own Google account so it can be restored on a new device.

Data processed by services we use

ServiceWhatWhyYour choice
Google AdMob (ads) Advertising ID, IP address (from which an approximate location can be derived), device and app information, ad interactions Showing a banner on the home screen, ads before some levels and optional rewarded ads for extra undos, which keep the game free; measuring and limiting ads; preventing fraud In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent first; change it any time in Settings → Ad privacy (shown where required). Without consent you get limited, non-personalised ads. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. Ads are limited to content rated for general audiences.

These services are provided by Google, which processes the data under its own terms: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services. Data may be processed outside your country, including in the United States, under safeguards Google provides (such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses).

Our own ads for other apps

When the ad network has no ad to show, the game may show a banner or a short full-screen video for one of our own apps (JazzIn Sudoku or JazzIn Block Puzzle) that is not yet installed on your device. The ad files are downloaded from trestas.github.io/ads/, a public page we host on GitHub Pages (like any website, it can see your IP address). The app checks which of our apps are installed only on your device and sends this list nowhere. Impressions and taps are recorded in Firebase Analytics without personal data. A tap opens the app's Google Play page.

Writing to us

The “Send feedback” button opens your own email app with a message to solocodelab@gmail.com; the game itself sends nothing. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and use it only to answer you and improve the game.

Legal bases (EEA and UK)

  • Personalised ads: your consent.
  • Non-personalised ads, analytics and crash reports: our legitimate interest in offering a free, working and improving game.
  • Emails you send us: our legitimate interest in answering you.

How long data is kept

Data on your device stays until you clear the app's data or uninstall it. Analytics data is kept for a limited period set in Firebase (no longer than 14 months); crash reports for up to 90 days. Google keeps ad data under its own policies.

Children

JazzIn Boulder is meant for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. If you believe a child under 13 has sent us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights and data deletion

To request deletion of your data, email solocodelab@gmail.com. You may ask to access, correct or delete your data, object to its processing or withdraw consent at any time. You may also complain to your data protection authority; in Lithuania this is the State Data Protection Inspectorate (vdai.lrv.lt).

Security

Data sent by the app travels encrypted (HTTPS).

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
