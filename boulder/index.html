JazzIn Boulder: Gem Puzzle

Every move changes the cave

Coming soon to Google Play

Dig through the earth, collect diamonds and find your way to the exit. The cave advances only when you make a move, so you can stop, study the rocks above you and choose your next step. Digging a passage can open a route – or release a falling boulder.

Think before you dig

Turn-based play: the cave moves when you do.

Collect gems and escape

Gather the required diamonds to open the exit, then find a safe way out.

25 cave levels

A campaign that goes ever deeper, with fireflies, butterflies and other cave dwellers.

Learn by playing

A short guide and an interactive demo cave with tips.

See the whole cave

Tap the top bar to view the entire level before you plan your route.

English and Lithuanian

The game follows your phone language.

How to play

The basics

Swipe or hold to move. You dig through earth as you go. A boulder or diamond falls when there is nothing beneath it – don't stand under it. Collect the required number of diamonds, then reach the exit.

Undo

Undo takes back moves. It is free in the first levels and a few times in every later level; after that a short ad unlocks it until your next move.

Free with ads

The game is free. It shows a banner on the home screen and a short ad before some levels.
