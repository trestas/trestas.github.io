Privacy Policy

Nonet (Android app, package com.sudokuplus) · Effective 5 October 2026

Nonet is a sudoku game. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, location, contacts or photos, and you do not need an account. This policy explains the little data the app does use, why, and how you can control it.

Who is responsible

SoloCodeLab, the developer of Nonet, is responsible for the app's data. Contact: solocodelab@gmail.com.

Data stored only on your device

Your games in progress, settings, statistics and daily streak are saved on your device. They are not sent to us. If Android backup is switched on for your device, Android may copy them to your own Google account so they can be restored on a new device.

Legal bases (EEA and UK)

  • Personalised ads: your consent.
  • Non-personalised ads, analytics, Remote Config and leaderboards: our legitimate interest in offering a free, working and improving game, balanced by the choices above.
  • Purchases: performance of the contract with you.

How long data is kept

Data on your device stays until you clear the app's data or uninstall it. Analytics data is kept for a limited period set in Firebase (no longer than 14 months). Leaderboard entries stay while the leaderboard is shown, or until you ask us to delete them. Google keeps ad data under its own policies.

Children

Nonet is meant for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. Ads are limited to content rated for general audiences (G and PG). If you believe a child under 13 has sent us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete your data, object to its processing or withdraw consent at any time. For leaderboard entries, send us your player name and the approximate date. You may also complain to your data protection authority; in Lithuania this is the State Data Protection Inspectorate (vdai.lrv.lt).

Security

Data sent by the app travels encrypted (HTTPS). Leaderboard rules only let each anonymous player change their own entry.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
